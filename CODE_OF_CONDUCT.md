# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, considerate, and open to feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and publication of others' private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. The maintainer may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation as appropriate.
